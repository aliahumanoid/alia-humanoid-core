    Friction Feedforward

    At low speeds, tendons exhibit stick-slip: the joint stalls until PID error overcomes static friction, then snaps forward. This adds a small torque in the direction of motion to pre-load against stiction.

    0 (off) 60 (aggressive)

    Below this → FF active (ramps to zero at threshold)
